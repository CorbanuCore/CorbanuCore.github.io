Bloom Energy is a U.S.-listed mid-cap growth equity whose dominant generic systematic driver is the broad U.S. equity market. The S&P 500 is the cleanest, most liquid broad equity index in the universe, with 24-hour perp liquidity of 89.219 USD millions versus BE's own 5.895 USD millions, making it the natural instrument to absorb generic market beta so the peer basket can isolate BE-specific economics. The Nasdaq-100 was considered but rejected because BE is NYSE-listed and is not a Nasdaq-100 constituent, so the S&P 500 is the more defensible systematic hedge.
