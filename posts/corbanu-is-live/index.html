Corbanu is live

2026-08-25 · Corbanu

Corbanu is a publication about on-chain speculation. Every article here is built around a trade: an instrument you can hold on-chain, an entry, a thesis, and the evidence behind it.

What you get

  • Market pages. Forty-one instruments across stocks, commodities, and crypto, each with a funding-inclusive perp history, a cash-reference total return line, a weighted peer basket, and live Hyperliquid pricing. Start at the markets table.
  • Significant moves. The front page ranks every instrument by how far it moved against its own peer basket over the trailing day. Divergence from peers is where the interesting questions live.
  • The newsletter. Trade ideas and research land in your inbox when there is something worth saying. Zero filler, zero daily commentary quota. Subscribe here.

How to read a market page

Each page shows two return histories on one axis: the cash reference with gross dividends, and the perp with every realized hourly funding payment. The peer table compares the instrument to its basket on price action, funding, and liquidity, with the blended peer average one row below the target. Supported stocks add earnings probability distributions, liquid option structures, and transcript briefings.

What comes next

Trade write-ups with tracked entries and exits, published here first and mirrored to the newsletter. Positions and referral relationships are disclosed on every post, and the standing policies live at corbanu.com/compliance.
