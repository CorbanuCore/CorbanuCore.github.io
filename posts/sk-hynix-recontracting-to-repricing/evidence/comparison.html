Definitions and coverage

  • P/E: Bloomberg BEST_PE_RATIO, BEST_FPERIOD_OVERRIDE=1BF. Positive observations only for P/E averages. Nonpositive/missing P/E is n/a, never zero. No winsorization or outlier removal.
  • First growth measure: 100 × (BEST_SALES 1BF / TRAIL_12M_NET_SALES − 1). The numerator and denominator are total company sales in the same reporting currency and Bloomberg units.
  • Second growth measure: 100 × (BEST_SALES 2BF / BEST_SALES 1BF − 1). 1BF and 2BF are blended forward periods; fiscal-year endpoints vary by issuer.
  • Trailing gross margin: TRAIL_12M_GROSS_MARGIN. Forward gross margin: BEST_GROSS_MARGIN with 1BF. Values already in percentage units. No substitution of operating margins.
  • The matched-currency PX_LAST / BEST_EPS arithmetic reproduces the direct P/E fields to better than 0.0001% relative error. For PDD, CCEP, FER and ASML, trading and earnings currencies differ; direct Bloomberg P/E handles that normalization, so an unconverted price/EPS quotient is not used.
  • All 101 company share-class rows resolved to Bloomberg names. They represent 100 issuers and 99.82% of supplied portfolio weight. Alphabet classes are combined at issuer level, using Class A fundamentals/P/E and summed allocation. The two class-specific P/Es remain in the full table/CSV.
  • The supplied table has 106 total rows. Non-company cash/futures/dividend rows are retained below and excluded from company multiples. Rounded supplied weights sum to 99.96%, not exactly 100%.
  • Undefined positive forward P/E: CoreWeave, Nebius, Rocket Lab, Warner Bros. Discovery. They remain in revenue-growth calculations; no earnings multiple is imputed.
  • “Technology” means Bloomberg GICS Information Technology: 42 issuers. Alphabet, Meta and other platforms in different sectors remain in all-QQQ averages and the selected-largest-issuers table.
  • Equal-weight averages use one observation per issuer; counts accompany each metric. Weighted arithmetic means renormalize supplied weights over valid observations. Neither statistic is an ETF aggregate P/E or a market-cap-weighted earnings yield.
  • Two exploratory bare-year override requests (2026/2027) returned missing EPS/P/E and identical quarterly-sized sales; these were rejected. Only explicit 1BF/2BF requests enter the displayed comparison.
