Quarter in one view

  • Record Q1 FY27 revenue $2.418B, +9% q/q, +28% y/y, above the $2.4B guide midpoint; non-GAAP EPS $0.80, $0.01 above midpoint (guide $0.74–$0.84). Data center $1.83B (76% of revenue), +11% q/q, +27% y/y — beat the ~+10% q/q guide.
  • Headline: another guidance raise, the third FY27 increase in three quarters — FY27 now "nearly $11.5B," +~40% y/y (from "approaching $11B" last quarter, ~$10B in December, ~$9.5B in September). Data center FY27 growth now ~50% (from ~40%).
  • FY28 raised again: ~$16.5B (+~45% y/y), ~$1.5B above last quarter's ~$15B; data center +~55% (from ~+50%), custom now "more than double" (from "at least doubling"), interconnect to keep outpacing cloud CapEx (moderating to "30% plus" growth).
  • Ramp cadence pulled forward: Q2 guide $2.7B ±5% (+12% q/q, +35% y/y), Q3 and Q4 each guided ≥+10% q/q; the ~$3B quarterly revenue milestone now lands in Q3 FY27, one full quarter ahead of plan; y/y growth accelerating to ~50% by Q4.
  • New strategic announcement: expanded NVIDIA partnership — three pillars: silicon photonics optics collaboration, NVLink Fusion integration (custom chips interfacing NVIDIA infrastructure), AI-RAN (OCTEON + NVIDIA GPUs); includes an NVIDIA equity investment (share count impact disclosed by CFO).
  • New acquisition: Polariton (plasmonic silicon photonics, >1 THz modulator bandwidth demonstrated) for DCI/coherent-lite roadmaps toward 3.2T. Record operating cash flow $639M; ~$1B of supply prepayments planned in FY27 starting Q2.

What management is focused on

  • The acceleration narrative itself: Murphy explicitly walked FY27 from ~$9.5B → ~$11.5B and framed FY28 at $16.5B with data center growth accelerating each year (46% FY26 → ~50% FY27 → ~55% FY28) — the message is bookings and "firm requirements" keep outrunning the model.
  • Interconnect as the star: FY27 interconnect growth raised to >70% (from >50% last quarter, ~30% original); Murphy called it "the star of the show" and attributed the raises to rising cloud CapEx plus rising attach rates.
  • NVIDIA partnership: positioned as validation and market expansion — NVLink Fusion gives hyperscalers "mix and match" flexibility with Marvell as "the bridge between these 2 architectures"; optics collaboration targets scale-up silicon photonics; AI-RAN extends OCTEON into GPU-integrated telecom.
  • Scale-up optics step-up: FY28 scale-up optics revenue now >2x the prior ~$150M Celestial-only outlook (i.e., >$300M, per Murphy's answer to Seymore), now including Marvell's own NPO/CPO products — "the beginning of a major growth cycle."
  • Supply/capacity as a strategic lever: ~$1B FY27 prepayments to lock capacity ("backing our forecast with confidence and cash," per Koopmans), replicating the prior supply-crunch playbook; Murphy credited supply chain execution as enabling the raise.
  • Custom long-term target reaffirmed: >$10B custom revenue in FY29 (re-anchored to the April 2024 targets and the June 2025 $55B TAM/~20% share math); new Tier-1 XPU is ~1/3 of FY28 custom growth, not the whole story.

Key numbers and quarter mechanics

  • Q1: revenue $2.418B; GAAP GM 52.1%, non-GAAP GM 58.9% (essentially flat vs. 59.0% in Q4, at the low end of the 58.25–59.25% guide); GAAP op margin 14%, non-GAAP 35% (down from 35.7%); non-GAAP opex $577M (vs. ~$575M guide); GAAP EPS $0.04 below guidance on Celestial/XConn purchase accounting and earn-out; non-GAAP EPS +29% y/y.
  • Data center $1.83B, +11% q/q, +27% y/y; comms & other $585M, +3% q/q, +29% y/y.
  • Cash flow/balance sheet: record operating cash flow $639M (vs. $374M in Q4); inventory ~$1.4B, "almost flat" q/q; debt $4.96B; gross leverage 1.44x, net 0.32x (improved from 0.57x); $200M buybacks + $54M dividends.
  • Q2 FY27 guide: revenue $2.7B ±5% (+12% q/q, +35% y/y); GAAP GM 52.1–53.1%, non-GAAP GM 58.25–59.25%; non-GAAP opex ~$600M (up from $577M); GAAP opex ~$960M; non-GAAP tax 11%; non-GAAP EPS $0.88–$0.98; GAAP EPS $0.32–$0.42; diluted shares 915M (up from 883M — full Celestial/XConn share impact plus NVIDIA investment shares).
  • FY27 non-GAAP opex ~$2.45B including acquisitions; FY28 opex guided mid-to-high teens growth vs. +45% revenue — management now expects the upper end of the 38–40% target non-GAAP operating margin model "as we progress through fiscal 2028."
  • Data center Q2 guide: mid-to-high teens sequential growth, mid-40% y/y. Comms & other Q2: mid-single-digit sequential decline, high-single-digit y/y growth.

Product and launch scorecard

  • Interconnect (>70% FY27 growth): 800G demand still strengthening; 1.6T ramping quickly with "another substantial step up" in FY28; 400G/lane next-gen demonstrated first at OFC April 2025. TIAs/drivers: on track to exceed $1B annualized quarterly revenue run-rate "in the next few quarters" — new quantification.
  • DCI: now shipping to all five major U.S. hyperscalers; FY26 revenue ~$500M (first quantification), line of sight to $1B annualized during FY28; secure 1.6T ZR/ZR+ modules on 2nm coherent DSP sampling this year (consistent with last quarter). Scale-across framed as the next driver with >10x bandwidth vs. current DCI.
  • Coherent lite: first-gen 1.6T shipping; next-gen with integrated MACsec now introduced — consistent with prior messaging.
  • Scale-up optics: FY28 revenue forecast more than doubled to >$300M (from ~$150M Celestial-only); Celestial chiplet selected by a Tier-1 hyperscaler for next-gen XPU scale-up (reaffirmed, no second named win); third-gen 6.4T light engine engaged with multiple Tier-1 customers; >1M SiPh DCI modules shipped, 15B field hours cited as credibility. Polariton adds plasmonics to the roadmap.
  • Switching: FY27 scale-out switch reiterated >$600M, doubling from FY26; now tracking to >$1B annualized in FY28 (new); 51.2T ramping, 100T platform seeing engagement (no timing update this quarter); 200T roadmap mentioned. Scale-up switching: UALink, ESUN, and NVLink Fusion all supported; multiple Tier-1 engagements, each framed as "multibillion-dollar lifetime revenue opportunity" — but Murphy explicitly said scale-up switching contributes "very little or nothing" in the $16.5B FY28.
  • Custom: FY27 >20% growth reiterated; FY28 now "more than double" (raised from "at least doubling") on three drivers sized roughly 1/3–1/3–1/3 (existing programs, 10+ XPU attach programs, new Tier-1 XPU ramp) — all three sized up since last quarter. New Tier-1 program "hitting all milestones" with firm requirements for all of next fiscal year. "Several new designs" won since last quarter (~2-year revenue lag, FY29 optionality). FY29 custom target >$10B reaffirmed.
  • AEC/retimers: still more than doubling combined in FY27; 3 Tier-1 U.S. hyperscaler AEC wins (unchanged); CXL attach alone still planned >$1B "in the next couple of years," with memory-cycle concerns driving additional CXL adoption (Curtis question).

Sell-side read-through

  • Arya (BofA): extracted explicit confirmation of the >$10B FY29 custom target and the math behind it ($55B TAM × ~20% share ≈ $11B); pressed on the new XPU customer disclosure timing and exclusivity — Murphy gave no timing commitment and no exclusivity answer, but sized the new program at ~1/3 of FY28 custom growth.
  • Arcuri (UBS): probed whether Marvell is moving into the largest "compute TAM" custom wallet; Murphy confirmed engagements across all U.S. hyperscalers but stressed the FY27–FY29 trajectory "is not requiring anything incremental" — new wins are an "insurance policy," FY29 at earliest.
  • Sur (JPMorgan): asked about SRAM-based LPU/offload inferencing ASICs (referencing NVIDIA's Groq-related move); Murphy validated the trend and Marvell's SRAM IP (2nm first last year, Avera/IBM/GLOBALFOUNDRIES lineage) but gave no design-win confirmation.
  • Seymore (Deutsche Bank): challenged the implied FY28 interconnect deceleration (>70% FY27 → ~CapEx-linked FY28) given all the tailwinds; Murphy conceded "definitely a possibility for a lot of upward bias," quantified FY28 scale-up optics at ~$300M, and hinted $16.5B has upside — a notably bullish admission.
  • Caso (Wolfe): asked whether the guide raise reflects newly secured capacity vs. conservatism vs. customer forecasts; Koopmans answered with the supplier-relationship/5-year-forecast/prepayments playbook — the mechanism was explained but the demand-vs-supply attribution of the raise was not directly answered.
  • Svanberg (Stifel): asked which 2–3 scale-up products are surprising to the upside; Murphy declined to rank products and pivoted to the end-to-end (XPU-to-switch) Celestial+Marvell integration story — breadth over specifics.
  • Pajjuri (RBC): scale-up switching sizing; Murphy said it's essentially not in the FY28 $16.5B at all — pure upside, deliberately excluded.
  • Tsvetanov (Wells Fargo, for Rakers): asked for detail on the new custom socket wins; Murphy refused specifics ("no additional details at this time").
  • Notable absences this quarter: no questions on gross-margin trajectory despite a second straight quarter at ~59%, no gross-margin bridge for the doubling custom mix, no Celestial lead-customer identity/AWS warrant follow-up (fourth call), no Q3 FY26 custom decline disclosure, no inventory or 1.6T/800G mix questions. Only 10 analyst questions; Murphy cut Q&A short ("one more question").

Management credibility

  • Delivered on the Q4 commitments: Q1 +9% q/q (guide +8%), data center +11% q/q (guide ~+10%), sequential-growth-every-quarter cadence intact — and accelerated it ($3B quarter pulled into Q3 from Q4).
  • The raise pattern is now three consecutive quarters (~$9.5B → ~$10B → ~$11B → ~$11.5B FY27; FY28 ~$15B → ~$16.5B), each attributed to bookings, firm requirements, and capacity secured. Directionally consistent with management's "conservative base case" posture, though the frequency of raises makes conservatism a less credible descriptor going forward.
  • Specificity improved again: first DCI revenue quantification (~$500M FY26, $1B run-rate FY28), first TIA/driver run-rate target (>$1B annualized within quarters), FY28 scale-up optics >$300M, custom FY28 split explicitly ~1/3–1/3–1/3 (answering Leopold directly), and explicit statement that scale-up switching is not in the $16.5B — a conservatism marker inside a big raise.
  • GAAP EPS missed guidance by $0.04 (purchase accounting/earn-out) — disclosed cleanly with normalization promised in Q2; a minor but real blemish to verify.
  • Persistent gaps: the Q3 FY26 custom decline has never been quantified (now four quarters); no custom-vs-merchant margin bridge despite custom doubling into FY28 while GM guides flat at ~59%; the new Tier-1 XPU still rests on "firm requirements" and internal milestones with no customer identity, PO disclosure, or exclusivity evidence (Arya pressed again, deflected again).
  • New execution dependency disclosed: ~$1B FY27 prepayments signal both confidence and that supply — not demand — is the binding constraint; Koopmans confirmed the AI supply chain "has not been unconstrained since 2020/2021." This is candid but raises the stakes on prepayment deployment.

What changed versus the prior quarter

  • FY27 revenue: "approaching $11B" → "nearly $11.5B" (+~40% y/y); data center ~+40% → ~+50%; interconnect >50% → >70%; FY27 EPS guide raised within the quarter (Q2 $0.88–$0.98 midpoint implies a strong H1 step-up).
  • FY28: ~$15B → ~$16.5B (+~45%); data center ~+50% → ~+55%; custom "at least doubling" → "more than double"; cloud CapEx assumption moderated to "30% plus" growth (from 60%+ in CY26).
  • Ramp timing: ~$3B quarterly revenue pulled forward from Q4 FY27 to Q3 FY27; Q2–Q4 sequential growth all guided ≥10% (prior: high single digit from Q2).
  • New items this quarter: NVIDIA partnership (optics/SiPh collaboration, NVLink Fusion, AI-RAN) plus NVIDIA equity investment; Polariton acquisition (plasmonics); ~$1B FY27 supply prepayments; DCI FY26 base (~$500M) and $1B FY28 run-rate; TIA/driver $1B annualized run-rate target; scale-up optics FY28 >$300M (>2x prior); scale-out switch FY28 >$1B annualized line of sight.
  • Operating margin framing: prior "target operating model exiting FY27" → "upper end of 38–40% as we progress through fiscal 2028" — modestly pushed out / reframed.
  • FY28 opex now guided mid-to-high teens growth (new disclosure).
  • Inventory stabilized (~$1.4B, flat q/q) and operating cash flow rebounded to a record $639M — resolving last quarter's two bear-case mechanics.
  • Still absent: AI >50%-of-revenue milestone (fourth call), Celestial customer identity/AWS warrant, Q3 FY26 custom decline magnitude.

Bull case

  • Guidance momentum is now self-reinforcing: three straight FY27 raises to ~$11.5B with Q2 +12% q/q, Q3/Q4 ≥+10% q/q, and y/y growth accelerating to ~50% by Q4 — plus a $16.5B FY28 frame Murphy openly said has "upward bias" (Seymore exchange).
  • Interconnect compounding above plan: >70% FY27 growth with multiple new quantified legs — DCI doubling to $1B annualized by FY28, TIAs/drivers crossing $1B annualized within quarters, scale-up optics >$300M FY28 (>2x prior), AEC/retimers more than doubling, switch to >$1B annualized in FY28.
  • NVIDIA partnership de-risks the scale-up and custom narrative: NVLink Fusion makes Marvell the bridge between custom and NVIDIA ecosystems, the optics collaboration validates SiPh scale-up, and the equity investment aligns incentives — plus scale-up switching ($0 in FY28) is pure upside across UALink/ESUN/NVLink.
  • Custom FY28 "more than double" now rests on three roughly equal, all-sized-up drivers; the new Tier-1 XPU (~1/3) has full-year firm requirements, and several new socket wins since last quarter create FY29 insurance against the >$10B target.
  • Financial engine improving: record $639M operating cash flow, inventory flat, net leverage down to 0.32x, upper end of the 38–40% margin model expected during FY28 on opex growing mid-to-high teens vs. 45% revenue.

Bear case

  • Gross margin has stalled at ~59% (58.9% in Q1, Q2 guided 58.25–59.25%) while the lowest-margin segment (custom) more than doubles in FY28 — the margin bridge to "upper end of 38–40% operating margin" is asserted via opex leverage alone; no analyst even asked the GM question this quarter.
  • The raise is increasingly supply-constrained execution, not just demand: ~$1B of prepayments starting Q2 concentrates working-capital and supplier risk; if demand normalizes, Marvell owns the capacity commitments; Koopmans conceded nothing AI-related has been unconstrained since 2020–2021.
  • Dilution is accelerating: diluted shares 915M guided for Q2 vs. 883M in Q1 and 857M a quarter earlier — Celestial/XConn shares plus the NVIDIA investment are outpacing $200M/quarter buybacks.
  • The new Tier-1 XPU remains the largest single unverifiable dependency: ~1/3 of a "more than double" custom year, no customer name, no PO disclosure, no exclusivity evidence, and Murphy declined to commit on when investors get more detail.
  • GAAP quality gap persists: GAAP EPS missed guide by $0.04 on acquisition accounting, GAAP op margin only 14% vs. 35% non-GAAP, and the gap will keep growing as Celestial/XConn/Polariton amortization layers in.
  • FY28 assumes cloud CapEx growth moderating only to "30% plus" while Marvell data center accelerates to 55% — a sharper decoupling from CapEx than last quarter's plan, raising the penalty if any digestion year arrives.
