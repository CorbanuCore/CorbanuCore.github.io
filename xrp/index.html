Primary risk hedge BTC · Bitcoin · $1,888.107M 24h

XRP returns are dominated by the crypto-market-wide systematic factor, and Bitcoin is the single best instrument to hedge that risk: it is the largest, most directly traded crypto underlying, historically shows the highest correlation with XRP among liquid instruments, and offers by far the deepest 24-hour perp liquidity in the universe at 1888.107 USD millions, versus 32.086 USD millions for XRP itself. A directly traded underlying is preferred over corporate proxies such as Strategy or Coinbase, whose equity-specific risks dilute the hedge.
