Designed around boundaries

Agent competence without standing authority

The workspace can help build and inspect systems. It does not custody assets, authorize orders, or replace venue-side permissions.

01 / CUSTODY

Assets stay at the venue

Your existing exchange or brokerage remains the custodian and source of account authority.

02 / CREDENTIALS

Secrets stay out of prompts

The encrypted vault exposes scoped capabilities to approved tools without placing keys in ordinary model context.

03 / EXECUTION

The trader controls the loop

Permissions are scoped at the venue. Any execution system connected to Corbanu output remains user-controlled.
