Primary risk hedge SP500 · S&P 500 · $50.729M 24h

SP500 is the cleanest primary risk hedge for XYZ100 because it shares the same US equity risk-on/risk-off channel, the same dollar and interest-rate discounting channel, and the same broad earnings cycle, while diluting the Nasdaq-100's extra concentration in mega-cap AI and semiconductor names. Its displayed 24-hour perp liquidity is 50.729 USD millions, close to XYZ100 at 52.585 USD millions, so the hedge can be sized with similar depth.

The construction is an AI and mega-cap growth sleeve rather than a generic basket. Nasdaq-100 returns are driven mainly by a small group of long-duration platform and semiconductor names, so the sleeve weights NVDA, Microsoft, Alphabet, Apple, Amazon, Meta, Micron, and Tesla according to economic comparability while using the displayed 24-hour perp liquidity stated in USD millions as a constraint. Micron has very deep displayed liquidity at 21.299 USD millions but receives only 0.07 because memory supply is an input-cost channel for the AI-hardware complex, not a full substitute for the whole index. Amazon is highly relevant through cloud and consumer-cycle exposure but is capped at 0.10 because its displayed liquidity is only 1.649 USD millions. The SP500 hedge is a correlated equity-beta substitute: it should offset part of broad index risk, but it will not perfectly protect a pure Nasdaq-100 technology concentration drawdown.
