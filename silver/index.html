Primary risk hedge GOLD · Gold · $23.982M 24h

Gold is the cleanest primary hedge for silver because both are precious metals driven by real interest rates, the US dollar, inflation-hedging demand and safe-haven flows; it offsets the monetary-metal component when silver's industrial beta is not the main driver. Its displayed 24-hour perp liquidity is 23.982 USD millions, close enough to SILVER at 32.581 USD millions to be executable while still being a distinct metal.

The block reserves GOLD as the primary hedge and uses peers to span silver's two engines: monetary-metal flow and industrial demand. COPPER carries the largest peer weight because it is the closest industrial-metal expression of the same global manufacturing and electrification cycle, even though its displayed 24-hour perp liquidity of 6.262 USD millions is far below SILVER at 32.581 USD millions. SP500 and CL add the broad risk-on/risk-off channel and the energy-inflation and mining input-cost channel with displayed 24-hour perp liquidity of 50.729 USD millions and 40.091 USD millions. SNDK, MU and TSLA are smaller end-demand proxies for electronics, memory, solar and electric-vehicle silver use; their displayed 24-hour perp liquidity is 98.839 USD millions, 21.299 USD millions and 4.787 USD millions, but single-name earnings and idiosyncratic risk keep their weights below the macro and commodity legs. Weights are positive, sum to exactly 1, and favor economic comparability first while using displayed 24-hour perp liquidity in USD millions as a constraint rather than the sole ranking signal.
