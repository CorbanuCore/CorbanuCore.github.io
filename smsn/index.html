Quarter in one view

  • Another record: revenue KRW 171.5T (+28% QoQ), operating profit KRW 89.5T (+56% QoQ), operating margin 52% (from 43%), net profit KRW 71.6T (+52% QoQ), EPS KRW 10,849 (+52%). FX added ~KRW 3.1T to operating profit (up from KRW 1.8T in Q1) — strip this when assessing underlying quality.

What management is focused on

  • Extending the supercycle timeline further: shortage now "persist through 2028" (vs. "widen in 2027" last quarter), with no meaningful incremental supply until after 2028 (3+ year fab lead times). New demand source named: "neo cloud" providers and AI frontier-model developers bypassing hyperscalers/OEMs to buy memory directly.

  • HBM mix inflection: HBM4 revenue +3x QoQ in Q3 guided; HBM4 >60% of HBM revenue in 2H (vs. ~half of full-year HBM stated last quarter — an upgrade); goal of HBM share "broadly in line" with overall DRAM share in 2H.
  • Incentive accrual now recognized: ~10.5% of cumulative 1H operating profit accrued in Q2, with explicit explanation that part is capitalized into WIP inventory and will hit COGS from Q3 — a forward cost headwind flagged.
  • Foundry momentum claims: 2nm wins from major CSPs, Broadcom named as in-talks, 2nm project wins to "more than double YoY" in 2026, AI/HPC revenue share from high-teens (2025) to >30% (2026), advanced nodes >50% of revenue, turnaround "possible in the near term."
  • New strategic pillars: robotics consolidated into CEO-reporting RX office with named hires (ex-Hyundai/Boston Dynamics EVP Dongkun Lee, two professors), Gumi pilot line; HARMAN acquisitions of ZF ADAS business and Sound United; "AI OS" repositioning of Galaxy AI; VD pivot to service/platform model under new leadership.

Bull case

  • Shortage visibility extended to 2028 with a physical mechanism (fab lead times), and unmet 2026 demand carrying into 2027 — pricing power persistence now argued on supply physics, not sentiment.
  • HBM inflection is near-term and quantified: HBM4 +3x QoQ in Q3, >60% of 2H HBM revenue, HBM share parity with DRAM in 2H, 2027 HBM supply agreements already signed — the catch-up thesis reaches proof point within one to two quarters.

Bear case

  • ASP growth decelerated sharply (+90s% → +40s/60s%); if this is the plateau phase of a price spike, 52% margins with KRW 3.1T FX help and capitalized incentive costs mark a peak-print setup. Mobile/PC demand destruction is already visible (volume down YoY, customer price increases).
  • MX is now losing money (−KRW 0.7T) — the internal tax of memory pricing is no longer a margin squeeze but an operating loss in the largest consumer business, with management conceding continued 2H cost pressure. Display and VD cite the same headwind.
  • Q2 profitability is flattered twice: KRW 3.1T FX gain plus incentive costs parked in WIP inventory that will hit COGS from Q3. Adjusted underlying momentum is weaker than the headline +56% OP.
  • The labor story is unresolved in disclosure: incentive accrual settled but the May–June strike and its production/cost impact were never addressed — silence on a previously disclosed 18-day general strike is itself a gap.
  • 60–70% capacity locked in 5-year contracts with price "floors" cuts both ways: if the cycle breaks, contracted volumes and floors protect Samsung, but capped flexibility and prepayment obligations constrain upside repricing and create counterparty concentration; terms are NDA'd and unauditable.
  • Escalating reinvestment into the peak: CapEx +5.5T QoQ to 16.8T, R&D +45% QoQ, Taylor Fab 2 committed to 2030 production, Pyeongtaek expansion — the 2028+ overshoot risk is being built while management asserts no meaningful new industry supply until then (an assumption that includes competitors' fabs).
  • Foundry turnaround remains a promise: "possible in the near term" with no absolute numbers; earnings "improvement before incentive-related charges" phrasing suggests charges still matter.
