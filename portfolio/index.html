      How the tracker works

      Cumulative P&L equals realized gains and losses plus the marked gain or loss on remaining quantities, plus recorded investment cash flows, less recorded fees, divided by initial capital. Adding or cutting a position changes its quantity; exiting preserves its realized result. Current marks carry their observation times. Missing marks leave the affected valuation unavailable.

      The portfolio source will specify whether funding, fees, dividends and borrowing costs are included.
