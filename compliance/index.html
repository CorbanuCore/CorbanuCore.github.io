Compliance & Disclosures

Corbanu is a publication. Tracked positions are the authors' own. Nothing on any Corbanu surface is individualized investment advice.

Author positions

It is disclosed explicitly that the authors or related accounts have entered positions prior to publication. This disclosure appears in clearly readable text at the end of every Corbanu article. Exits can follow large price movements, valuation changes, or discretionary changes, with prompt notification in the originating article. These policies are stated in advance, in plain English.

Referral links

Venue and product links in Corbanu content can be referral links that carry compensation. Every referral link is disclosed as such. Compensation never determines whether a trade enters the book, and payout rates are never inputs to trade ranking. Venues without referral programs remain eligible.

Sponsorships

Sponsored content is visibly labeled. Sponsors never review trade conclusions before publication, and sponsorship is never presented as an independent trade initiation.

Performance and track record

Published trades carry entry prices and, once closed, exit prices, so the record is verifiable against the claims. Performance figures state whether they are gross or net, include losses without selective removal, and describe public reference prices rather than private account results.

Corrections

Material errors are corrected promptly and visibly. Corrected records are preserved rather than silently overwritten.
