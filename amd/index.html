Quarter in one view

  • Q2 FY2026 revenue $11.5B, +50% y/y, +13% q/q — beat the ~$11.2B ±$300M guide, landing near the top. Non-GAAP GM 56% (in line with guide; +200bps+ y/y, +80bps q/q), EPS +~82% y/y on a comparable basis, opex $3.4B (+40% y/y, ~$100M over the $3.3B guide), operating income $3.1B (27% margin). Note: y/y comparisons exclude ~$800M of Q2 FY2025 MI308 China export-control charges.
  • Data Center record $6.7B, +107% y/y, +16% q/q — now 58% of revenue (42% a year ago). Server CPU: fifth straight record, cloud AND enterprise each +70%+ y/y (beat the guided +70%+ overall). Instinct: more than doubled y/y on MI350-series ramp.
  • Client & Gaming $3.8B, +6% y/y (Client $3.1B +23% y/y, record mobile; Gaming $779M, -31% y/y on semi-custom decline and Radeon softness). Embedded $977M, +19% y/y — strongest growth in 3+ years.
  • Strategic news: Anthropic partnership — up to 2GW of MI450 in Helios, first GW deploying 1H27, plus a Claude-based joint engineering collaboration; expanded Microsoft partnership deploying Helios at scale on Azure. Cerebras partnership (Helios + wafer-scale engine) for fast inference, available in Cerebras cloud from Q4.
  • Q3 guide: ~$13B ±$300M (+41% y/y, +13% q/q), GM ~56%, opex ~$3.65B, other income ~$55M gain, tax 13%, ~1.66B shares. DC and Embedded guided up strong double digits q/q; Client & Gaming modest decline (slight client growth, strong double-digit gaming decline).
  • Big guidance escalation: server CPU +80%+ y/y in 2H26, +70%+ for FY2027; total DC segment to "well over" double in 2027. TAMs raised again: AI accelerators to ~$1.4T by 2030 (+45%/yr), server CPU to ~$220B by 2030 (+50%/yr) — the second TAM revision in nine months.

What management is focused on

  • Server CPU as a second structural engine: Su now quantifies 2H26 (+80%+ y/y) and 2027 (+70%+ y/y off a higher base), with unit growth outpacing ASP growth (Q2: double-digit growth in both, units higher). Supply chain (wafers, back-end, substrates) being expanded; supply tight in 1H26 because demand was "unforecasted," expected better in 2027.
  • Helios/MI450 ramp mechanics: in production now (Su mentions "MI455X" in production — a new product identifier not previously disclosed), initial shipments later this quarter, step-up in Q4, further step-up in Q1 2027, ramping through 2027. Customer pull "tracking ahead of our initial forecast" — clarified as volume/demand-driven, not yield-related.
  • 2027 DC AI ramp framing: "well over 100%" segment growth; revenue-per-gigawatt reiterated as "double-digit billions" — Su did not endorse Arya's ~3GW/$15B-per-GW math but called supply sufficient "to more than meet" guidance with upside.
  • TAM escalation: overall high-performance/AI compute market ~$2T by 2030 growing ~40%/yr; AMD expects to grow "well above the market" and "materially ahead" of the November Analyst Day model — revenue "substantially above" the >35% CAGR target, EPS to "significantly exceed" $20.
  • Venice momentum: in production, >30 SKUs, OEM platforms and cloud deployments starting later this year; claimed >2x perf/watt vs leading x86, up to 3.3x vs leading ARM CPUs. Demand "stronger than any prior EPYC generation."
  • ROCm inflection: 3M+ models run out-of-box, day-0 support for leading open models, open-source contributions up 10x y/y; ROCm.ai platform (Claude/Codex/Cursor agents) claims >2x training and 3x inference performance vs ROCm 7. Co-optimization with OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta.
  • Opex leverage commitment (new, direct): Hu committed opex growth below revenue growth going forward — after another guide overshoot ($3.4B vs $3.3B) and a $3.65B Q3 guide (+$250M step-up).

Key numbers and quarter mechanics

  • DC $6.7B (+107% y/y, +16% q/q); op income $2.1B = 31% margin (up from 28% in Q1). EPYC +70%+ y/y with double-digit unit AND ASP growth (units-led); Turin now powers ~1/3 of 1,600+ EPYC public cloud instance types; 230+ 5th-gen EPYC OEM platforms. Instinct >2x y/y on MI350 ramp across AI labs, CSPs, start-ups, national labs, sovereigns. No Instinct revenue figure disclosed; no China commentary at all this call.
  • Client $3.1B (+23% y/y, +6% q/q), record mobile revenue; Ryzen Pro +50%+ y/y sell-through momentum continuing. Gaming $779M (-31% y/y, +8% q/q on semi-custom up / Radeon down); component-cost-driven GPU pricing weighing on demand. C&G op income $582M = 15% margin (down from 21% a year ago on GTM/roadmap investment).
  • Embedded $977M (+19% y/y, +12% q/q); op income $386M = 40% margin (33% a year ago). Embedded x86 "grew significantly" on hyperscaler/networking control-plane adoption; tracking >$18B of new design wins for the year (record).
  • GM 56% (+80bps q/q); Hu's 2027 framework: server CPU accretive, DC AI slightly below corporate average (she said "slightly below" — softening from prior "below"), embedded recovery an additional 2027 tailwind. Q3 GM guided ~56% — flat despite Helios start, implying modest initial dilution.
  • Opex $3.4B vs $3.3B guide (overshoot again, ~4th consecutive). Q3 guided $3.65B. Cash from ops $2.4B; FCF $1.6B (down from $2.6B in Q1 — inventory build to $8.5B from $8.0B to support DC ramp). Cash + ST investments $13.1B (up from $12.3B).
  • Q3 guide mechanics: +41% y/y / +13% q/q at midpoint; DC and Embedded strong double-digit q/q growth; Client & Gaming down modestly (client slight growth, gaming strong double-digit decline). Helios revenue begins late Q3, "much more substantial" in Q4 (per Su, answering Arcuri).

Product and launch scorecard

  • Helios/MI450: in production; initial shipments "later this quarter" — the Q3 start from last quarter is intact. Demand "ahead of initial forecast" on volume, not yields (Su, under Buchalter). Claims up to 15% more throughput at same rack power and up to 30% more tokens/dollar vs competition. Anthropic (2GW, 1H27 first deployment) and Microsoft Azure scale deployment are new named proof points; OpenAI/Meta reaffirmed. Su conceded yields will improve over the first few quarters — a margin caveat for early ramp quarters.
  • MI355X: carried the >2x y/y Instinct growth; still no revenue number.
  • MI500 (new disclosure): 2027 rack-scale platform with Verano CPUs, Pensando networking, expanded copper AND optical scale-up domains; "largest generational leap in Instinct history"; multiple customers engaged. Annual rack-scale cadence formally committed.
  • Venice: in production (ahead of "launch later 2026" language from Q1); >30 SKUs; every major OEM on track; hyperscaler deployments starting later this year; Su claims workload breadth expands vs Turin.
  • Ryzen AI Halo: next-gen "Gorgon Halo" platform introduced (192GB unified memory, up to 300B-parameter local models), Hugging Face Pro bundled 1 year — developer/edge AI positioning rather than mainstream PC.
  • Embedded: Ryzen AI Embedded x100 and Kria robotics platform launched; embedded x86 becoming "a significant growth driver."
  • Cerebras partnership: Helios + wafer-scale engine for fast/disaggregated inference, Cerebras cloud availability from Q4 — first concrete answer to the low-latency inference question Arcuri pressed last quarter.

Sell-side read-through

  • O'Malley (Barclays) tested the CPU/GPU crossover and 2027 GM mix; Su declined a crossover timing but said both grow strongly with AI the larger TAM; Hu gave a clean 2027 GM framework (CPU accretive, DC AI slightly below average, embedded a tailwind) — notably more constructive on GM than prior quarters.
  • Arcuri (UBS) forced the Q3→Q4 shape: both server and DC AI grow each quarter, Helios only "just starting at the end of Q3" and "much more substantial in Q4" — i.e., the Q3 guide is largely CPU/MI355-driven. Also got Su to commit to growing revenue above the ~40% TAM CAGR through 2030.
  • Arya (BofA) tried to pin GW visibility (~3GW, ~$15B/GW): Su declined to confirm the math, reiterated "double-digit billions per gigawatt," and said supply exceeds guided needs with upside. On HBM (~50% more than competition): "very good visibility into HBM allocation" for 2027; memory footprint can be trimmed per workload — a flexibility claim worth testing against BOM economics.
  • Buchalter (TD Cowen) got the units/ASP split (units > ASP, both double-digit) and extracted that "ahead of forecast" = volumes, with yields still ramping — an implicit early-GM caution.
  • Rakers (Wells Fargo): Su sized the agentic/"sandbox" server bucket as smallest today but the largest and fastest-growing piece of the $220B TAM — meaning the TAM revision leans on a nascent category.
  • Rasgon (Bernstein) flagged that "accelerating in 2H" is a 2H-vs-1H statement (Hu confirmed), not vs Q2's 107% — guide implies ~80s% DC y/y in Q3. Pressed the "more than" in the 2027 targets: Su said DC AI will be "well over 100%" — escalation in response to pressure, still without numbers.
  • Muse (Cantor) floated ~$30B 2027 Instinct revenue; Su said his number "is probably too low" — the most directional management has ever been on DC AI scale, without confirming any figure.
  • Moore (Morgan Stanley) confirmed server CPU supply is tight now (unforecasted demand), expected better in 2027; Su said >70% 2027 growth could go higher.
  • Malik (Citi): Cerebras detail; Hu's opex-leverage commitment ("opex increase less than top-line growth") — the most direct response yet to Rasgon's standing opex critique, and testable next quarter against the $3.65B guide.

Management credibility

  • Delivery: beat guide top-half ($11.5B vs $11.2B ±$300M); server CPU delivered +70%+ in both cloud and enterprise against the guided +70%+ overall — the aggressive Q1 call validated. GM hit 56% as guided. Fifth straight record server quarter; every guided segment shape (DC/Embedded up double digits, C&G modest) appears to have printed.
  • New named customers materially de-risk MI450: Anthropic (2GW, engineering collaboration) and Microsoft (Azure at scale) join OpenAI and Meta — four named at-scale anchors plus "additional customers" at sub-GW scale claimed for Q4/Q1. This is the strongest evidence yet behind the 2027 ramp.
  • Opex credibility still the weak spot: $3.4B vs $3.3B guide — the fourth consecutive overshoot — while guiding +$250M more for Q3. Hu's new explicit "opex grows slower than revenue" commitment now gives the street a measurable test; the $3.65B guide vs +41% y/y revenue growth is directionally consistent for the first time.
  • TAM revision cadence is becoming a pattern: server CPU TAM went $60B (Nov) → $120B+ (Q1) → $220B (Q2) in nine months; accelerator TAM to $1.4T. Su's bottom-up framing is consistent, but the revisions now underpin the "well over 100%" 2027 and "significantly exceed $20 EPS" claims — assertion-based until Q4 shipments land.
  • Evasions/opacities persist: still zero Instinct revenue disclosure (Muse's $30B got a wink, not a number); no ex-China base ever quantified; no China/MI325 commentary at all; GW visibility question not answered numerically. Language escalation ("well over," "materially ahead," "significantly exceed") is running ahead of disclosed data.
  • MI455X naming appeared without explanation — either a new SKU or loose language; worth clarifying.

What changed versus the prior quarter

  • Two new named at-scale customers (Anthropic 2GW, Microsoft Azure Helios) plus Cerebras — vs Q1, when the open question was converting "additional multi-gigawatt opportunities." That conversion has started.
  • 2027 visibility quantified for the first time: server +70%+ y/y, DC AI "well over 100%," total DC more than doubling; revenue-per-GW reiterated as double-digit billions. Prior quarter offered only "tens of billions" and >80% CAGR aspiration.
  • TAMs raised again: accelerator to ~$1.4T (+45%/yr) and server CPU to ~$220B (+50%/yr) by 2030; total AI/HPC compute ~$2T. Analyst Day model declared outdated: revenue "substantially above" >35% CAGR, EPS "significantly exceed" $20.
  • Helios moved from "sampling" to "in production, shipments this quarter"; MI500/Verano 2027 platform disclosed with annual cadence commitment — new roadmap concreteness.
  • Server CPU trajectory sharpened: +80%+ 2H26 (vs guided +70%+ for Q2 alone last quarter), +70%+ for FY2027; supply tightness explicitly acknowledged as the binding 2026 constraint.
  • Gaming shifted from "2H down >20% vs 1H" caution to a realized -31% y/y and guided strong double-digit q/q decline in Q3 — the consumer headwind is now in the numbers.
  • Embedded acceleration delivered (+19% vs +6%), design wins >$18B — upgraded from "billions."
  • Opex: another guide overshoot, but a new formal leverage commitment from Hu. FCF dropped to $1.6B on inventory build for the DC ramp ($8.5B vs $8.0B).
  • GM narrative improved: Hu now calls DC AI "slightly below" corporate average and frames 2027 GM constructively, vs prior deflection.

Bull case

  • Beat-and-raise with accelerating magnitude: $11.5B actual, $13B Q3 guide (+41% y/y) — a $1.5B sequential step-up — with GM holding 56% through the Helios start.
  • Server CPU is a validated, quantified second engine: +70%+ delivered in both cloud and enterprise, +80%+ guided for 2H26, +70%+ for 2027, units-led (share gain, not just pricing), supply additions landing through 2026-27, Venice in production with "strongest demand of any EPYC generation."
  • MI450/Helios demand is now concretely multi-customer: OpenAI, Meta, Anthropic, Microsoft at scale, plus sub-GW customers for Q4/Q1; demand ahead of plan; revenue per GW "double-digit billions"; management actively steering the street's 2027 Instinct numbers UP (Muse exchange).
  • Software moat building: ROCm at claimed inflection (3M models, 10x contributions, agent-based tooling, co-optimization with the three biggest labs), Anthropic engineering deal embeds Claude in ROCm development.
  • GM mix math now works on paper: CPU + embedded accretion offsetting "slightly below average" DC AI; Q3 56% guide held despite Helios start.
  • Balance sheet supports the ramp: $13.1B cash, record design wins ($18B+), embedded x86 opening a new DC-adjacent growth vector.

Bear case

  • Opex discipline still unproven: $3.4B vs $3.3B guide (fourth straight overshoot), $3.65B guided for Q3; the leverage commitment is new and untested.
  • The boldest claims remain disclosure-free: no Instinct revenue, no backlog, no GW totals confirmed, no China discussion; "well over 100%" 2027 growth rests entirely on management assertion and customer forecasts.
  • TAM whiplash risk: server CPU TAM tripled in nine months ($60B→$220B) with the largest bucket ("agentic sandboxes") admittedly the smallest today — if agentic AI spend normalizes, both the TAM and the >50% share/$20+ EPS narrative compress.
  • Consumer drag is real and worsening: gaming -31% y/y and guided down strong double digits q/q in Q3; 2H PC market soft on memory costs; C&G margin already down 6pts y/y to 15%.
  • FCF halved q/q ($2.6B→$1.6B) on inventory build to $8.5B — working capital will absorb cash through the Helios ramp; margin and FCF quality need watching as rack-scale (lower-GM, HBM-heavy) revenue mixes in.
  • Early-ramp execution risk: Su conceded Helios yields are still improving; complex rack-scale ramps at four simultaneous anchor customers (with CSP intermediation and data-center readiness dependencies) leave room for timing slips into 2027.
  • 2027 is now heavily pre-committed in language; any customer pushout (power, shells, networking) hits a stock-priced trajectory.

Next-quarter watchlist

  • Q3 print vs $13B ±$300M / 56% GM / $3.65B opex: does opex land at/below guide for the first time in five quarters (Hu's new leverage test); does R&D vs SG&A growth confirm the prior commitment.
  • Helios first revenue: shipment confirmation within Q3, initial customer mix (Schneider's Q4/Q1 diversity question), early GM commentary vs "slightly below corporate average," yield/ramp commentary after Buchalter's question.
  • Server CPU: does +80%+ 2H26 trajectory hold; evidence of supply additions landing; any ASP pushback as inflation passes through; Venice OEM/cloud launch execution later this year.
  • 2027 framing: whether "well over 100%" DC growth survives scrutiny at Citi (Sept 8) and Goldman (Sept 11) conferences; any quantification of GW commitments (OpenAI/Meta/Anthropic sum) or revenue-per-GW.
  • China: complete absence of commentary this call — any resumption or new license language would be a change.
  • Memory: HBM allocation for 2027 vs the "very good visibility" claim; whether GPU Radeon demand and PC 2H softness track the guided decline or worsen.
  • Embedded: whether the $18B design-win year converts into sustained double-digit growth; embedded x86 control-plane traction as a margin tailwind into 2027.
  • Clarify the MI455X reference and any product segmentation within MI450 series.

  • Financial momentum: 36% growth with rising GM and tripling FCF funds the +42% opex investment without balance-sheet strain.

Bear case

  • Customer concentration: OpenAI could approach half of DC GPU revenue by 2027–28 per UBS framing; the deal's economics are partially equity-linked (warrants), and OpenAI's own power/CapEx/funding constraints (raised by Arya) sit outside AMD's control.   • Margin dilution risk: DC operating margin already fell y/y (25% vs 29%) on R&D; management declined to give any 2026 GM framework and prioritized GM dollars over percentage — rack-scale systems historically carry lower margins.

  • 1H26 transition risk: growth depends on MI355 sustaining until MI450 ramps 2H26; any digestion creates a gap management only partially addressed.
  • China MI308 remains unresolved — excluded from guide, licenses partial, demand environment unclear.
  • Embedded still declining y/y with falling margins; gaming faces a strong double-digit sequential drop in Q4; opex +42% y/y raises the bar for operating leverage if revenue growth decelerates.
